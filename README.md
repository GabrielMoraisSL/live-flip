# LiveFlip 🛹

**LiveFlip** is a private social network built for a select group of skaters to track and share their progression through tricks and achievements.

## About

The idea is simple: a personal, underground space where skaters can post videos of their tricks, unlock achievements by landing maneuvers, and build a profile that reflects their real journey on the board — no algorithms, no noise, just skating.

Access is restricted by login, keeping the community tight and intentional.

## Features

- **Video feed** — chronological posts of tricks and skate-related content, with simple likes and comments
- **Achievement system** — an extensive list of tricks organized by stance (Base, Switch, Nollie, Fakie) and 3 progressive difficulty levels. Unlock an achievement by posting a video of the trick and linking it to the corresponding conquest
- **Skater profile** — public profile featuring bio, base stance (Regular or Goofy), local spot, and a full list of unlocked achievements
- **Conquest posts** — when a post is linked to an achievement, it gets a visually distinct card in the feed

## Culture

> *"De onde você é local?"*

The concept of **local** — the spot where you skate most — is a core part of every skater's identity here. Each profile carries that culture forward.

Achievements aren't validated by anyone. It runs on the crew's honesty. If you post it, you landed it.
